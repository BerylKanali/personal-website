Fellowships & Programs

UbuntuNet Alliance Women Hackathon – Team Kenya

2023 | Kampala, Uganda

Selected to participate in the first UbuntuNet Alliance Women Hackathon, which brought together women developers from across Eastern and Southern Africa to develop technology solutions for research and education.

As part of Team Kenya, I worked with Jane Jackline Waithira and Rose Caleno to develop NovaNex, a collaborative learning and knowledge-exchange platform.

Selected Participant – R Project Sprint 2023

2023 | University of Warwick, United Kingdom

Selected to participate in the R Project Sprint 2023, a contributor sprint that brought together R contributors and members of the R Core Team to collaborate on contributions to the R programming language.

The sprint provided an opportunity to contribute within the wider R community, collaborate directly with experienced contributors, and deepen my understanding of the development and contribution processes behind an established open-source software project.

KamiLimu Mentee & Fellow

Cohort 6 Mentee | Fellow, 2022–2023

My journey with KamiLimu began as a mentee in Cohort 6, where I participated in the program's structured mentorship experience alongside other university students in Kenya.

Following the cohort, I was selected as a KamiLimu Fellow. The fellowship provided an opportunity to give back to the program by serving the following cohort and contributing to the committee responsible for supporting the mentorship experience.

This experience marked an important transition from being a beneficiary of the program to helping create the experience for others. I later continued with KamiLimu in a leadership capacity, taking on responsibility for its ICT and Innovation work.
