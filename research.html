Research

Publications

Feature Selection-Assisted Support Vector Machine Classification of Breast Cancer Mammographic Outcomes

This research explores the use of machine learning for classifying mammographic breast cancer outcomes. The study examines how Correlation-Based Feature Selection and Elastic Net can be used to identify relevant features from mammographic image data before classification using Support Vector Machines.

The work brings together medical imaging, statistical methods, feature selection, and machine learning to explore approaches for breast cancer classification.

Authors: Beryl Kanali, Pius Kihara, and Anthony Karanjah

Published in: Asian Journal of Probability and Statistics, Volume 28, Issue 10, 2026

Nurturing Responsible Student Tech Innovators Through Out-of-Classroom Upskilling in Kenya

This research examines how responsible computing can be integrated into out-of-classroom technology upskilling and innovation programs for university students in Kenya.

Drawing from the KamiLimu model, the paper explores a pathway from Responsible Computing to Responsible Innovation, showing how mentorship, project-based learning, and innovation activities can encourage students to consider the wider social and ethical implications of the technologies they develop.

Authors: Chao Mbogho, Beryl Kanali, and Mark Tanui

Published in: Proceedings of the ACM Global Computing Education Conference 2025, Volume 1
