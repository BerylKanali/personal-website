Publications

Nurturing Responsible Student Tech Innovators Through Out-of-Classroom Upskilling in Kenya

ACM Global Computing Education Conference (CompEd) 2025, Vol. 1

Using the KamiLimu model, the paper sets out a pathway from responsible computing to responsible innovation. It shows how mentorship, project-based learning and innovation activities help students weigh the social and ethical implications of what they build.

Authors: Chao Mbogho, Beryl Kanali and Mark Tanui

Read the paper

Feature Selection-Assisted Support Vector Machine Classification of Breast Cancer Mammographic Outcomes

Asian Journal of Probability and Statistics, Vol. 28, Issue 10, 2026

Examines how Correlation-Based Feature Selection and Elastic Net can identify relevant features in mammographic data before classification with Support Vector Machines, bringing together statistics, feature selection and machine learning for health.

Authors: Beryl Kanali, Pius Kihara and Anthony Karanjah

KamiLimu Fellow

Fellow 2022 – 2023 · Committee member since 2023

KamiLimu is a structured mentorship programme for computing students in Kenya. I was a KamiLimu Fellow in 2022–2023, then joined the committee in 2023. I have led its innovation work ever since, with a new cohort of 40 students each year.
