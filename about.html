About

Community builder, programme designer and researcher, working where technology meets people.

Who I am

I trained in mathematics and computer science and came into open source as an Outreachy intern with Bioconductor. Since then my work has moved from the code towards the people around it: how communities welcome newcomers, make decisions and keep going.

Today I coordinate the global OpenMRS digital health community, and I lead innovation work at KamiLimu, where students learn to turn ideas into responsible, usable solutions. I also research how that kind of out-of-classroom learning works.

I now offer this experience as an independent consultant. My training spans mathematics, statistics and engineering and technology management, so I can work as easily with technical teams as with the people who fund and run programmes.

What I believe

Communities are infrastructure

Software lasts only as long as the people who maintain and use it. Investing in contributors is investing in the product.

Responsibility from the start

Ethics, safety and impact on communities belong in the first workshop, not in the last review.

Ideas need a path to use

Prototypes and hackathon wins are a beginning. The real work is testing, improving and getting solutions adopted.

Explain it simply

If the people affected cannot understand a technical decision, it is not finished yet.
