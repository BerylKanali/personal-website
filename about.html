About Me

Profile and professional focus

Who I Am

I am a technology and community professional working at the intersection of open-source technology, digital health, innovation, and education. My work is centred on building communities, programs, and systems that help people participate meaningfully in technology and use it to solve real-world problems.

I currently work as the Community Coordinator at OpenMRS, where I support a global open-source digital health community of contributors, implementers, partners, and organisations. My work spans community coordination, contributor engagement, program delivery, communications, partnerships, and initiatives that strengthen the OpenMRS ecosystem.

I also serve as the ICT & Innovation Lead at KamiLimu, where I work with university students and early-career technologists on innovation, responsible computing, technical product development, and applying technology to real-world challenges.

Across my work, I am particularly interested in how technology communities are built and sustained, how innovation moves from an idea to something useful, and how emerging technologies such as AI can be applied responsibly and inclusively.

What I Care About

Open Source & Digital Health

Building strong contributor and implementer communities around technologies that improve health systems and access to care.

Innovation

Helping people move from identifying meaningful problems to developing, validating, and improving technology-based solutions.

Responsible Computing

Exploring how technology can be designed and deployed with greater consideration for people, communities, ethics, and societal impact.

Community & Learning

Creating environments where people can learn, contribute, collaborate, and develop the confidence to participate in technology communities.

Beyond Work

Outside work and study, I enjoy reading, lifting weights, playing basketball, travelling, and exploring new ideas and experiences.
