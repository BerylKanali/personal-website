What I Do

My work sits at the intersection of open source, digital health, technology and innovation, community building, and education. I work with technical communities and teams to turn ideas into programs, products, learning experiences, and initiatives that create meaningful impact.

I currently work across global open-source digital health, technology mentorship and innovation, while also pursuing research interests in machine learning, responsible computing, and technology management.
