What I have built, the problems behind it and what changed.

OpenMRS · 2024 – present

Keeping a global digital health community connected

The challenge

OpenMRS is built by a distributed community of contributors, squads and implementing organisations across many countries. Their priorities need to stay aligned with one product and community roadmap.

What I did

I coordinate the programmes that join these groups up: contributor onboarding, Google Summer of Code, roadmap reviews, partner meetings and community events. I also run community communications and reporting so everyone can see priorities and progress.

Outcome

A community of 500+ contributors and implementing partners with clearer pathways for contributing, and community meetings that bring 250+ people together around demos, implementation lessons and the roadmap.

Related service: Community strategy · Digital health delivery

KamiLimu · 2023 – present

Teaching students to innovate responsibly

The challenge

Computing students in Kenya learn the technical skills but rarely get the chance to apply them to real problems, or to think through the social and ethical effects of what they build.

What I did

After my KamiLimu Fellowship, I joined the committee in 2023 and have led innovation since then. As the first Fellow to lead both the ICT and Innovation dockets, I designed the innovation curriculum and cohort calendar: problem identification, validation, human-centred design, responsible computing and pitching. In 2026 I added a Buildathon.

Outcome

A cohort of 40 students every year since 2023, 40+ teams taken from problem to pitch, and 86 students in the 2026 Buildathon. The model was published at the ACM Global Computing Education Conference 2025.

Related service: Innovation programme design · Read the paper

KamiLimu Responsible Innovation Hub · 2026 – present

Taking student innovations beyond the prototype

The challenge

Promising student projects often stop after a competition or demo day. Without structured support they never get tested with real users or adopted.

What I did

I lead the design of the Hub's structure, programming and support model. It brings technical guidance, mentors and partners to teams, and builds responsible computing into every stage from validation to adoption.

Outcome

A new incubation pathway at KamiLimu, now being built and piloted.

Related service: Innovation programme design

Astropy · 2023 – 2024

An engagement strategy for scientific open source

The challenge

Astropy is maintained by a global, volunteer-heavy community of astronomers and developers. Participation was hard to see, and so were the community's engagement priorities.

What I did

As Lead Community Manager, I created and implemented an engagement strategy, tracked participation to guide priorities, and reviewed the Code of Conduct.

Outcome

A data-informed approach to engagement and an updated Code of Conduct that strengthens contributor safety and inclusion.

Related service: Community strategy
