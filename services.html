Services

Programme and innovation roles, advisory and project work, for organisations building innovation programmes, technology communities and learning initiatives.

Where I add value

I work with organisations that need someone who understands both the technology and the people around it, whether in a programme or innovation role on your team or on a defined project. Everything here draws on work I do every day, not theory.

Innovation programme design & incubation

For: universities, incubators, funders and organisations running innovation programmes

What I can do

  • Programme and curriculum design: cohort calendars, learning tracks, milestones
  • Problem identification, validation and human-centred design sprints
  • Incubation models that move teams from prototype to pilot and adoption
  • Building responsible computing into the whole innovation lifecycle
  • Pitch preparation, demo days and judging frameworks

Track record: KamiLimu ICT & Innovation track, the KamiLimu Responsible Innovation Hub and peer-reviewed research on the model. See research

Programme & project delivery

For: organisations running multi-partner programmes, technology projects and initiatives that need to land well

What I can do

  • Planning: scope, workplans, timelines and milestones everyone signs up to
  • Day-to-day delivery: tracking progress, managing risks and dependencies, unblocking teams
  • Coordinating partners, implementers and technical teams around a shared roadmap
  • Monitoring and reporting, with metrics and executive updates that show progress against goals
  • Planning and running convenings, partner meetings and events
  • Closing out well: evaluation, lessons learned and handover

Track record: Programme coordination across the OpenMRS digital health ecosystem, including Google Summer of Code; running KamiLimu's yearly innovation cohort and the 2026 Buildathon. Backed by postgraduate training in engineering and technology management. See work

Open-source community strategy

For: open-source projects, foundations and digital public goods

What I can do

  • Community health assessments: who participates, where people drop off, what blocks contribution
  • Contributor onboarding pathways and mentorship programmes (e.g. Google Summer of Code, Outreachy)
  • Engagement strategies with metrics you can track and report on
  • Governance and Code of Conduct review
  • Community communications: newsletters, updates and reporting to leadership

Track record: OpenMRS community coordination, Astropy community management, Bioconductor contribution through Outreachy. See work

Training, facilitation & speaking

For: teams, universities and communities building technical capacity

What I can do

  • Workshops on open-source contribution and collaboration workflows
  • Responsible computing and responsible innovation sessions
  • Science communication and technical storytelling for non-technical audiences
  • Facilitation of strategy sessions, retrospectives and design workshops
  • Talks and panels on open source, digital health and innovation in Africa

Track record: 90+ students trained in science communication; open-source training for KamiLimu cohorts.

Also available: research & data support

With an MSc in Mathematics, Science and Statistics and published machine-learning research, I can support evaluation design, data analysis and evidence-gathering for programmes and products.

How I work

  1. ConversationWe talk through your goals, your context and what success would look like.
  2. DiagnoseI review what exists and talk to the people involved, then share clear findings.
  3. DesignWe agree a plan, a strategy or a programme with milestones you can measure.
  4. Deliver & hand overI deliver the work and leave your team with the tools to keep it going.
